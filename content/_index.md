Hi! I am a fourth year PhD student at the [Department of Applied Mathematics and Statistics](https://engineering.jhu.edu/ams/) at Johns Hopkins University, where I am fortunate to be advised by [Soledad Villar](https://www.ams.jhu.edu/villar/) and [Tim Kunisky](http://www.kunisky.com/). My research interests lie in developing a better understanding of neural networks, including their approximation, generalization, optimization, and scaling behavior, using a variety of mathematical tools from algebra, probability theory, analysis, and topology.
My current research focuses on the following topics and related areas:
* Size generalization (training on small inputs, with the goal of performing well on large inputs)
* Hyperparameter transfer (tuning hyperparameters on small neural networks and using them on large ones)

Check out the [research](https://yuxinma98.github.io/research/) page to learn more.

Previously, I completed an [MMath and BA Hons degree in Mathematics](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/undergrad/undergrad) at the University of Cambridge where I was mentored by [Benedikt Löwe](https://www.math.uni-hamburg.de/home/loewe/) and [Orsola Rath Spivack](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/or100/). During my master's studies, I focused on statistics and completed an [essay](https://1drv.ms/b/c/e03f4172bb13adf9/EfmtE7tyQT8ggOCNmQAAAAABhfVBbB6fDku2oiO60cYQzw) on Topological Data Analysis, advised by [John Aston](https://www.statslab.cam.ac.uk/~jada2/) and [Jacob Rasmussen](https://www.dpmms.cam.ac.uk/~jar60/).
